# Security and privacy

Use verified accounts for swaps, server-side authorization, scoped operator roles, protected sessions, rate limits, safe uploads and encrypted transport. Minimize access to contact information, messages, tracking and dispute evidence; retain audit records without secrets. Support reporting and blocking, and provide clear instructions for direct handoffs. Safeguards reduce risk but do not guarantee fraud prevention. Establish retention, incident response, fee refund, item loss and custody policies before launch.
