# Egypt-focused legal register

**Assessment date:** 27 November 2026. **Purpose:** technical issue-spotting for Next Pull, an Egypt-focused online commerce/community service. This is not legal advice or a formal compliance opinion. Have Egyptian counsel verify Arabic Official Gazette text, amendments, business-specific licensing, and each determination below.

## Article-level anchors verified for working register

- Consumer Protection Law 181/2018, Arts. 3–10, 17–24, 28–29, 35, 37–41. WIPO Lex English Official Gazette copy shows: Art. 17 general 14-day return/exchange right without expenses subject to exceptions; Art. 21 30-day remedy for defective/non-conforming/unsuitable goods without additional cost; Arts. 37–41 remote-contract disclosures, acceptance/order correction and confirmation, distance withdrawal and delayed delivery. The Ministry of Finance official Law 20/2024 record links the enacted amendment; its published text changes Art. 8 penalties. Counsel must confirm the Arabic consolidated wording, exceptions, Art. 17/40 shipping cost interaction, regulations and CPA decisions before publication.
- PDPL 151/2020: promulgating Arts. 1–7 (scope, exceptions, transition and commencement); attached Law Arts. 1–18 (definitions, consent/lawful grounds, controller/processor obligations, rights, transfers, direct marketing); Arts. 19 onward (Center, permissions and enforcement). For final pinpoint citations, use the official Arabic PDF and official Decree 816/2025; an English translation is not authoritative.
- E-Signature Law 15/2004: Art. 1 definitions and Arts. 14–18 recognition/evidentiary conditions (verify exact article application to ordinary web acceptance in counsel review).
- Child Law 12/1996, Art. 2, as amended: child means under 18. Do not infer a uniform “age of digital consent” or contracting capacity from that definition alone.

## Scope outside Egypt

GDPR applies only if its territorial criteria are met, including an EU establishment or offering goods/services to people in the EU or monitoring their behavior there (GDPR Art. 3). Project materials describe Egypt as the primary market; source does not establish EU targeting or EU-resident users. Do not apply GDPR wholesale absent evidence. If triggered, assess GDPR duties for EU data subjects, lawful basis, transparency, rights, processors, transfers and representative/DPO requirements. [Official GDPR text, EUR-Lex](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj).

Other foreign laws: no facts establish US/California, UK, Brazil or other market targeting or relevant thresholds. Reassess if geography, customer targeting, corporate presence, or processing changes.
