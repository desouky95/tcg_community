# Legal compliance gap analysis

Egypt-focused issue-spotting based on `docs/legal/audit/`. No legal conclusion or legal advice. Severity is prioritization, not a penalty assessment.

| Priority | Gap and evidence | Applicable source / applicability | Risk | Corrective action |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Critical | No evidenced PDPL notice, lawful-basis register, complete processing record, data-role map or PDPC permit/licence review. Personal data spans users, guests, addresses, messages, OAuth and orders. | PDPL 151/2020; Decree 816/2025; Egypt-focused processing. | Processing could lack required transparency, accountability or authorization; active legal regime’s transition has elapsed. | Egyptian privacy counsel to classify operations and determine controller/processor, lawful bases, notices, registers, DPO, licences/permits. |
| Critical | Hosting and processor regions/contract terms unknown; S3-compatible storage, OAuth providers and DB configured. | PDPL Arts. 14–15 and Decree 816/2025 if cross-border processing occurs. | Transfers/storage could be unauthorized or lack required protection/permit. | Map country/legal entity/subprocessor for each flow; obtain PDPC approval/permits and transfer/consent mechanism as counsel determines. |
| Critical | No consumer pre-sale disclosure, durable confirmation, clear legal seller identity, invoice evidence, return/refund/warranty operations established. | Consumer Law 181/2018 Arts. 3, 9–10, 17–24, 29, 37–41; Decree 822/2019. Law 20/2024 amends Art. 8 penalties per official published amendment. | Consumer disputes, order terms not provable, statutory remedies mishandled. | Publish required seller/product/price/delivery/warranty details; order confirmation; staffed returns and defect/counterfeit complaint procedure. Counsel must confirm Art. 17/40 shipping-cost relationship, current Arabic text and exceptions. |
| Critical | Children may use collection/social/shop features; no age handling in source. | Child Law Art. 2 (<18); PDPL child-data rules/regulations; contractual capacity. | Child profiling, unsafe contact/swaps, invalid/contestable transactions. | Product owner chooses audience. Counsel assesses consent/capacity. Add age-appropriate defaults, guardian process or age restriction and safety measures accordingly. |
| Critical | Account deletion/export does not prove deletion in Medusa, CMS, Spaces, backups or messages; no retention schedule found. | PDPL rights, purpose limitation/storage controls; consumer/accounting retention may qualify. | Rights requests incomplete; unnecessary retention or erasure of records that must be preserved. | Data-store-by-store retention matrix, propagation, legal holds and backup expiry; exercise DSAR in staging, log outcomes. Counsel approves retention exceptions. |
| High | Marketing/cookie implementation and consent status unknown. | PDPL direct electronic marketing Art. 18/regulations; identifier data covered by PDPL. | Unlawful direct marketing or untransparent tracking if external tags/campaigns operate. | Audit deployed pages, outbound traffic and CRM; ensure marketing opt-in/proof/withdrawal and cookie notice/control based on counsel’s reading. |
| High | Seller/intermediary role and UGC marketplace governance unresolved; user messaging/swaps and card listings are implemented. | Consumer Law and IP Law 82/2002; obligations vary by business model. | Counterfeit/unauthorized imagery, misleading listings, harmful content, disputes and address leakage. | Define seller-of-record vs marketplace, seller onboarding/identity, condition/authenticity representations, complaint/takedown, safety moderation. Counsel review. |
| High | No proof of required security management, breach notification procedure, DPO, processor agreements or rights contact. | PDPL/Decree 816/2025. | Incident response or mandatory records/notifications missed. | Confirm current Arabic executive rules and PDPC operational guidance; assign owner, prepare incident and DSAR runbooks, contracts and access controls. |
| High | Checkout supports guest data and shipping; actual courier, payment and support processors unknown. | PDPL; Consumer Law disclosure/contract duties. | Personal data passed to unidentified vendors; guest access or retention may be insecure. | Inventory vendor recipients and contract terms; disclose recipients/purposes; secure guest order access; minimize exported address copies. |
| Medium | Electronic order acceptance/correction workflow and durable order confirmation not demonstrated. | Consumer Law Art. 38; E-Signature Law 15/2004. | Disputes about order terms and consumer correction rights. | Add/verify review, correction and durable confirmation; preserve terms/version and acceptance timestamps. |
| Medium | Card authenticity and intellectual-property provenance unclear. | Consumer Law product conformity; IP Law 82/2002. | Counterfeit goods and unauthorized card artwork use. | Product-level provenance/condition, image rights and claims escalation. |
| Medium | Current amendment/version uncertainty for Consumer Law Art. 38/40. | Law 20/2024 amendment surfaced in research; official consolidated current text not verified. | Wrong return-period or checkout disclosures if relying on unamended translation. | Egyptian counsel obtains gazette amendment and consolidated text before operational terms. |
| Low | GDPR not established on current facts. | GDPR Art. 3; Egypt-primary positioning, no evidence of EU targeting/monitoring. | Mis-scoping GDPR or overlooking it after expansion. | Confirm geo-targeting, EU campaigns, monitoring and establishment annually or at expansion. |

## What this assessment does not establish

It does not decide whether Next Pull is legally compliant, identify all current licenses, determine controller status, establish applicability of each consumer remedy to each collectible, determine a valid age threshold for independent contracting, or confirm live production configuration. Those require current Arabic legal text, business facts and Egyptian counsel.
