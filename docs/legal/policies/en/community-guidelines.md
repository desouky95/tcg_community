# Community Guidelines

**Draft for legal review — not approved for publication** · Version 0.1 · Proposed effective date: 27 November 2026

Next Pull supports collector profiles, collections, reviews, conversations and card swaps. These guidelines help keep those spaces useful and respectful. TODO(confirm): approved community support/reporting contact.

- Treat collectors respectfully. No harassment, threats, hate, bullying, sexual exploitation or targeted abuse.
- Keep private information private. Do not publish another person’s contact details, delivery address, account data or private messages without permission.
- Be accurate about cards and trades. Describe edition, language, condition, defects and authenticity honestly. Do not claim official certification or authenticity without evidence.
- Do not promote counterfeit goods, stolen property, unlawful copies or unauthorized card scans/artwork.
- Keep swaps consensual and clear. Agree on the cards, condition, delivery method and any costs before sending. Do not pressure users to move conversations or payment outside safe channels.
- Protect minors. Do not solicit private contact, images, payment or in-person meetings from a child. Report concerning contact through TODO(confirm): reporting channel.
- Post only content you own or have permission to use. Do not upload personal information or images of others without a lawful basis/permission.

Use the in-product reporting mechanism if available; its production availability is unverified. Do not include sensitive details in a public report. Next Pull may restrict content or accounts under the Acceptable Use Policy; its review and appeal procedures need confirmation.

These guidelines do not replace consumer rights, trading terms or legal reporting routes. Business confirmation required for moderation coverage, escalation, appeal and response practices.
