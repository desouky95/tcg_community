# Cookie Policy

**Draft for legal review — not approved for publication** · Version 0.1 · Proposed effective date: 27 November 2026

Next Pull uses browser cookies for sign-in/session state, authentication security and cart continuity. The repository evidences Adonis session/XSRF cookies, Better Auth cookies and a storefront cart cookie. Medusa also configures long-lived rolling sessions. Actual production names, attributes, durations and domains must be checked before this policy is published.

## Cookie categories

- **Strictly needed for requested features:** session/authentication cookies and cart identifier cookies. Without them, account access or cart/checkout continuity may not work.
- **Security:** XSRF/session controls help protect authenticated requests.
- **Analytics/advertising:** No active analytics or advertising cookie was established by the repository review. This is not confirmation that production pages, a CDN, tag manager or third-party scripts do not set them.
