# Pre-order Policy

**Draft for legal review — not approved for publication** · Version 0.1 · Proposed effective date: 27 November 2026

Next Pull’s catalog supports product availability types that include pre-order. This policy applies only when a product page clearly identifies an item as a pre-order before checkout.

## Before ordering

Each pre-order listing must state the expected release/dispatch window, item and edition details, total price and any deposit/balance arrangement, delivery charges, and what happens if the estimate changes or supply is unavailable. The repository does not establish a standard release estimate, deposit, payment schedule, cancellation workflow or supplier allocation. **TODO(confirm):** define these commercial terms per listing before offering a pre-order.

An estimate is not a guaranteed date unless the listing expressly says so and the seller can meet it. If supply or timing changes, the seller should promptly tell the buyer and explain available choices, subject to mandatory law.

## Cancellation, refunds and consumer rights

No pre-order term may waive mandatory Egyptian consumer rights. Cancellation, withdrawal, delayed delivery, non-supply, defects and refunds are governed by applicable law and the Refund and Return Policy. Whether a specific made-to-order or sealed collectible exception applies must be confirmed for that item; pre-order status alone does not create an exception.
