## 2. Information we process

Depending on your use of Next Pull, the service may process:

- **Account and sign-in information:** username, name, email, mobile number, password verifier, account status, language, governorate, profile preferences, verification and last-login information. Passwords are represented in the API model through a password-hashing mechanism.
- **Social sign-in information:** provider account identifier and profile claims returned by Google or Facebook if you choose those configured sign-in options. The exact live providers, claims and scopes require confirmation.
- **Orders and delivery:** guest or account email, recipient name, phone, address, city, country, cart, products, order and payment-state information needed to complete a purchase or delivery.
- **Collector and community information:** collection/checklist progress, reviews, messages, conversations, swap proposals and swap status. Messages may include personal details you choose to share.
- **Preferences and technical records:** privacy/communication preferences, session and cart identifiers, access-token metadata, request/operational records and information needed to protect and operate the service. The complete log and telemetry inventory is not yet verified.
- **Content and media:** content or images you submit, or staff upload to the CMS. Do not submit another person’s personal information or material you lack permission to share.

The service supports guest checkout; a guest does not need an account for every purchase. Checkout data is still personal information.
