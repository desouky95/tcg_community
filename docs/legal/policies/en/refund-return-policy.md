## Statutory rights

The 2018 law text provides several distinct protections that must not be collapsed into a single “no returns” rule:

- Article 17 generally gives a consumer the right to replace or return a good and receive its cash value within 14 days of receipt without expenses, subject to the statutory exceptions and any CPA rules for specific goods.
- Article 21 gives a 30-day right to replace or return a good with a refund if it is defective, does not conform to specifications, or is unsuitable for the purchased purpose, without additional cost, subject to statutory rules.
- Article 40 separately governs distance contracts, including a 14-day withdrawal right, exceptions under Article 41, refund timing/method, and delayed delivery. Its return-shipping rule appears different from Article 17’s no-expense rule.

Law 20/2024 is in force; the official Ministry of Finance record and the law’s published summary concern amendments to penalties for Article 8, rather than changing these return provisions. The available English 2018 law is not an official consolidated Arabic text. Egyptian counsel must confirm current Arabic wording, the interaction and scope of Articles 17, 21 and 40, Executive Regulations and any CPA commodity-specific decisions before publication. No term in this policy limits a mandatory consumer right.

Defective, unsafe, counterfeit, materially misdescribed or non-conforming items must be handled under applicable statutory remedies. A card’s collectible nature or market value does not by itself establish an exception. Packaging condition may matter under specific statutory exceptions; do not apply an opened-product exclusion unless counsel confirms the statutory facts and exception for that item.
