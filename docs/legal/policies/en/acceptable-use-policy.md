# Acceptable Use Policy

**Draft for legal review — not approved for publication** · Version 0.1 · Proposed effective date: 27 November 2026

This policy applies when using Next Pull accounts, collections, reviews, messages, swaps, marketplace and uploads. TODO(confirm): identify the legal operator and support/reporting contact.

You must not:

- break applicable law or infringe another person’s rights;
- impersonate a person, seller, rights-holder or Next Pull, or provide materially deceptive account/listing information;
- list or trade counterfeit, stolen, unsafe or materially misdescribed cards/items;
- upload or distribute content without the necessary rights, or content that is unlawful, threatening, harassing, abusive, exploitative or exposes another person’s private information;
- use messaging or swap features for spam, scams, coercion, unwanted solicitation or unsafe contact, especially with children;
- interfere with service security or availability, access another person’s account, evade access controls, scrape restricted data, or send malware;
- misuse personal information received through a transaction, message or swap.

Share only information needed for a transaction. Do not post payment credentials, passwords, one-time codes, identity documents, or another person’s address/phone publicly. Use official order/payment instructions; Next Pull’s repository currently evidences an offline InstaPay option, but live methods must be confirmed.

We may review reports and take proportionate action, including removing content or restricting access, subject to applicable law and fair review.
