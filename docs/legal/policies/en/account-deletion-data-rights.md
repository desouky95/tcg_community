# Account Deletion and Data Rights

**Draft for legal review — not approved for publication** · Version 0.1 · Proposed effective date: 27 November 2026

The collector app provides controls to request a personal-data export and account deletion. The current deletion implementation anonymizes selected Adonis account fields and cancels pending exports; it does not establish complete deletion from Medusa commerce records, messages, CMS media, vendors, logs or backups.

## Request an export or deletion

Use the privacy controls in account settings where available, or contact TODO(confirm): privacy contact. We may ask for information reasonably needed to verify account control. Do not email passwords, one-time codes or full payment credentials.

Export files are designed to expire after seven days. The actual cleanup operation and export coverage must be confirmed. Protect any downloaded export because it may contain account and activity information.

## What deletion means

After a verified request, Next Pull will process the request under applicable law. Some account fields may be anonymized, and some records may remain where needed for an applicable legal, accounting, dispute, security or other lawful purpose. The current system’s full cross-service deletion behavior, backup lifecycle and retained record categories are not confirmed. We will not promise immediate or universal erasure in this draft.
