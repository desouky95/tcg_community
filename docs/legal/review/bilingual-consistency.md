# Bilingual legal-document consistency review

**Internal review.** Compared EN/AR policy and Terms pairs for scope and material consumer/privacy clauses. This is not a certified translation or Arabic legal review.

| Document pair | Result | Observed differences / action |
|---|---|---|
| Terms and Conditions | Substantively aligned after correction. | EN says “direct-to-consumer” style; Arabic follows meaning. Both include account/guest, commerce, random contents, swaps, UGC/IP, suspension, mandatory rights and placeholders. Translate the final Arabic legal terms consistently and choose precedence after counsel review. |
| Privacy Policy | Broadly aligned. | Both include same data categories, unknowns, rights/deletion limits, child/cross-border uncertainty and security caveat. Arabic wording “إخفاء/استبدال” should be legally checked against anonymization; avoid implying validated irreversible anonymization. |
| Cookie Policy | Broadly aligned. | Both identify session/auth/cart cookies, unknown tags and no verified preference center. Confirm technical cookie terminology and legal consent phrasing in Arabic. |
| Refund and Return Policy | Updated to include Arts. 17, 21 and 40–41 in both languages. | Both flag the cost interaction and exceptions; counsel must validate Arabic statutory text and avoid any English/Arabic difference on who bears costs or refund timing. |
| Shipping and Delivery Policy | Broadly aligned. | Both mark unknown courier, territory, costs, tracking, estimates and damage process. Avoid final publication until same service details supplied. |
| Pre-order Policy | Broadly aligned. | Both conditional; no unsupported deposits, deadlines or non-refundable claims. Confirm same live feature and listing disclosures. |
| Account Deletion and Data Rights | Broadly aligned. | Both say export designed for 7-day expiry and deletion is not universal; Arabic must not overstate “إخفاء” as full deletion. |
| Acceptable Use Policy | Broadly aligned. | Conduct rules and absent moderation/reporting/appeals are consistent. Review terms for children and user-content restrictions in Arabic. |
| Community Guidelines | Broadly aligned. | Both avoid claiming a report mechanism is live and require contact confirmation. |

## Cross-document terminology

- “Operator” → “المشغّل”; “seller/supplier” → “البائع/المورّد” depending on the transaction. Counsel must choose exact Arabic statutory term and not treat operator and supplier as always identical.
- “Consumer” → “المستهلك”; “user/collector” → “المستخدم/الهاوي”. Preserve the statutory meaning of consumer.
- “Pre-order” → “الطلب المسبق”; “Coming Soon” → “قريباً”; “swap” → “مبادلة”. Confirm product UI terms and consistent translations.
- “Anonymize” should not translate to a term implying irreversible anonymization unless technical validation proves it. Current deletion implementation overwrites selected account identifiers; use “إزالة/استبدال بعض بيانات الحساب” pending validation.
- English and Arabic drafts remain parallel documents, not official translations. Egyptian counsel must compare against Arabic Official Gazette requirements and select a controlling version.
