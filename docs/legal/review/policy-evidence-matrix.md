## Consistency decisions

- “Next Pull” refers to the service/brand only; legal operator remains `TODO(confirm)`.

- Drafts do not set invented retention, contact, delivery, return-processing or commercial refund periods.
- Legal consumer rights are not waived by the drafts. A statutory timeline is stated only as a qualified summary where the 2018 text supports it; current amended Arabic text must be confirmed.
