# Commercial decisions required before approving Terms

**Internal working document — not customer-facing.** Business decisions below are not decided by repository evidence. Legal counsel should review decisions marked legal.

| Decision | Evidence/constraint | Decision owner | Required decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| Operator and seller identity | Legal name, legal form, registration, address and contact absent from supplied repository evidence. Consumer distance-sale disclosures require supplier identity details. | Business leadership + Egyptian counsel | Confirm entity, commercial registration, address, tax details and support/legal contacts; identify seller of record per transaction. |
| Platform role | Code supports Medusa shop sales plus Adonis community swaps; whether third-party sellers transact is unknown. | Business leadership + counsel | State if Next Pull sells its own stock, hosts third-party sellers, facilitates peer trades, or combinations; allocate responsibility accurately. |
| Account age/eligibility | No age field or gate found; Child Law defines child under 18, but independent capacity requires legal analysis. | Product + Egyptian child/privacy counsel | Choose intended audience, age threshold by feature, guardian consent and safety model. |
| Signup/verification | Email/mobile/password and OAuth flows exist; OTP routes point to disabled handler; email verification not evidenced. | Product/engineering | Confirm available methods and whether user must verify email/mobile. Terms must describe the live behavior only. |
| Guest orders | Guest checkout captures email, recipient name, phone and address. Guest order lookup/access process unclear. | Commerce/product | Decide guest confirmation, support verification, order access and account linking. |
| Product condition/authenticity | Catalogue and individual card sales exist; no independent authentication/grading process established. | Inventory/marketplace operations | Adopt condition grades, seller claims/evidence, counterfeit workflow, return inspection and use of grading-company marks. |
| Sealed randomized products | No confirmed inventory classification/pull-odds feature in evidence. | Commerce + counsel | Confirm whether sealed booster/packs are sold; source/accuracy of odds; listing language and handling for tampered/opened items. |
| Missing-list products | Backend workflow creates individual card variants with inventory and EGP prices; card purchase supported. | Commerce | Confirm listing accuracy/stock, substitutions, partial fulfillment and cancellation for unavailable individual cards. |
| Collection records/digital ownership | Collection progress exists; digital collectible/NFT/ownership transfer not evidenced. | Product | Confirm digital items are not offered. If intended later, create separate legal/product review before launch. |
| Orders and contract formation | Checkout creates/places orders; precise acceptance event/confirmation/version logging not established. | Commerce + engineering + counsel | Select order acceptance point, review/correction flow, confirmation contents, order cancellation conditions and durable evidence. |
| Payment | Custom offline InstaPay provider in EGP configured; live methods and manual reconciliation unknown. | Finance + commerce | Confirm methods, payment window, reconciliation, duplicate/late payment, failed payment, refunds, fees and provider details. |
| Shipping | Address/fulfillment selection exists; courier, geography, charges, estimates, tracking and damage process unknown. | Operations | Set service locations, courier, delivery estimates/charges, tracking, address changes, missed delivery, loss/damage escalation. |
| Returns/refunds/warranty | No workflow or commercial period in source. Statutory rights cannot be waived; Consumer Law consolidation/amendments need counsel. | Commerce/support + counsel | Decide additional commercial benefits beyond law; confirm procedure, return cost, destination, payment-specific refund handling, exchange and warranty terms. Do not invent statutory exceptions. |
| Pre-orders | Catalog supports product availability labels; “Coming Soon” is blocked from purchase. Active pre-order flow and terms not evidenced. | Commerce + counsel | Confirm if pre-orders are purchasable, release estimates, allocation, deposits/balance, supplier delay, cancellation/refund and unavailability remedies. |
| Swaps | Deal supports postal/in-person types, accept, tracking/photo and receipt/QR states. No guarantee, escrow, insurance or adjudication evidenced. | Community/product + counsel | Decide whether platform merely facilitates, what support/mediation exists, participant eligibility, safe meeting guidance, shipping loss and dispute handling. |
| User content license | Profiles, reviews, messages, swap content and media exist; no terms license/retention. | Product + counsel | Approve narrow operational license, deletion effect, sublicensing to infrastructure providers, publicity use (default no unrelated use), retention and content-removal process. |
| Moderation/suspension | Policy surfaces exist, but reporting/appeals/moderation service not established. | Trust/community operations | Define report channel, urgent safety handling, review, appeal, enforcement grounds, notice and open transaction treatment. Avoid service promises until staffed. |
| Intellectual property process | Card images/brands and CMS media present; ownership/source and rights notice process unknown. | Brand + counsel | Confirm owned/licensed images, publisher mark usage, user upload rights, counterfeit review and notice/takedown contact/process. |
| Governing law/forum | Egypt-primary market; legal entity/forum not specified. | Egyptian counsel | Approve governing law, competent forum, consumer complaint path, language precedence and whether any dispute process is appropriate. No arbitration waiver by default. |
| Terms changes/acceptance evidence | No verified change-notice/re-consent process or accepted-version log. | Product/engineering + counsel | Decide clickwrap/checkout acceptance, version archive, logging, notices, timing and reacceptance triggers. |
| Liability language | No established entity or tested legal allocation. Statutory consumer rights must remain. | Egyptian counsel | Draft any disclaimer/cap/indemnity to fit Egyptian law; no cap inserted in current Terms draft. |

## Prioritized sign-off

Before publication: resolve operator/seller role, age policy, returns and order contract formation, actual payment/shipping/pre-order model, and complaint contacts. Then confirm authentication/guest behavior, sealed product scope, swap role, moderation/IP processes and terms acceptance evidence. Counsel must approve both language versions and checkout presentation.
