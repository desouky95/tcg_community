## Cross-document consistency check

- Terms do not override rights described in Refund and Return Policy, Shipping and Delivery Policy, Pre-order Policy, Privacy Policy, Cookie Policy, Account Deletion and Data Rights Policy, Acceptable Use Policy or Community Guidelines.
- All documents use unresolved operator, contact and delivery data as TODOs; no fabricated business identity/address/contact appears.
- No blanket exclusion for opened card products, pre-orders, digital products or refunds.
- Do not publish until Arabic text, current Egyptian consumer-law amendments, child capacity/PDPL rules, seller role and actual procedures receive legal/business approval.
