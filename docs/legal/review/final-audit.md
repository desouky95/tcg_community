# Final independent audit of Next Pull legal documentation

**Review date:** 27 November 2026 · **Status:** internal review, not legal advice. **No code changes, publication, or legal approval.** Scope: all generated legal policies, terms, audit and compliance documents, relevant source paths, registration/checkout UI evidence, and authoritative sources available during this review.

## Executive result

The drafts are **not ready for publication**. They are suitable for legal review after the documented corrections, but material facts remain open: operator/seller identity, current business practices, checkout acceptance/confirmation, processor list and locations, age policy, and operational return/shipping paths. None is represented as legally approved.

The most significant corrected documentation omission was consumer return rights: the earlier policy set centered Article 40 distance withdrawal and omitted the general Article 17 14-day right and Article 21 30-day remedy for defective/non-conforming goods. English and Arabic refund and Terms drafts, and the legal register/matrix, now include those separate rights and flag the Article 17/40 return-cost interaction for Egyptian counsel. Law 20/2024’s official published amendment changes Article 8 penalty provisions; prior wording suggesting it might have changed Article 38/40 was corrected.

## Legal references checked

- Egypt Consumer Protection Law 181/2018: English Official Gazette copy via WIPO Lex supports Arts. 17, 21, 28–29, 35 and 37–41. [Official Gazette copy](https://wipolex-res.wipo.int/edocs/lexdocs/laws/en/eg/eg065en_1.pdf). Law 20/2024 is listed as in force by the Ministry of Finance and its official linked text changes Article 8 penalty provisions: [Ministry of Finance record](https://mof.gov.eg/ar/legislation/legislation-6643253a8897b900092f6319).
- Egypt PDPL 151/2020 and Decree 816/2025: the docs cite the official Arabic law/PDPC resources; English operational interpretation and exact licensing, child and transfer requirements still require counsel. [PDPC official portal](https://portal.pdpc.gov.eg/guidelines).
- Child Law No. 12/1996, as amended: Article 2 definition under 18 supported by Cabinet-hosted English text. This does not resolve contract capacity. [Cabinet-hosted source](https://egcert.eg/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/ChildLawno126english_eng.pdf).
- E-Signature Law 15/2004 source is ITIDA; ordinary order acceptance effect remains fact-specific. [ITIDA law text](https://itida.gov.eg/English/Documents/law-no-15-of-2004-itida-e-signature.pdf).
- IP Law 82/2002 source is linked in legal register; precise takedown/intermediary duties require counsel. [WIPO Lex record](https://www.wipo.int/wipolex/ar/legislation/details/22066).
