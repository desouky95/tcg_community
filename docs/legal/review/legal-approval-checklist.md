# Legal approval checklist

**Internal; complete with Egyptian counsel and responsible business owners.** No item is legally approved by this checklist.

## Business identity and model

- [ ] Verify legal entity name, legal form, commercial registration, tax details, address, website and monitored customer/privacy/legal contacts.
- [ ] Classify the operator as seller, marketplace, facilitator, or combinations for shop products, third-party listings, individual cards, missing-list sales and peer swaps.
- [ ] Identify seller identity disclosures, invoice/receipt process and complaint route required for each model.
- [ ] Confirm intended age audience and legal capacity/guardian requirements per signup, purchase, messaging and swap.

## Egyptian consumer law

- [ ] Counsel checks Arabic Gazette text of Law 181/2018, Executive Regulations Decree 822/2019, Law 20/2024 and any later amendments/CPA decisions.
- [ ] Approve distinct descriptions of Article 17 general returns, Article 21 defective/non-conforming goods, and Articles 37–41 distance contracting.
- [ ] Resolve return-cost interaction between Articles 17 and 40; identify only legally valid product-specific exceptions, including sealed/opened packaging circumstances.
- [ ] Verify delivery delay, cancellation, refunds, order correction/confirmation, invoice, warranty and used-goods requirements.
- [ ] Review products: opened packs, sealed randomized products, pre-orders, individual cards, “Coming Soon” and any future digital item. No blanket exclusion without clear authority.
- [ ] Ensure required information and contractual documentation are available in Arabic; decide legally controlling language and review translation fidelity.

## Privacy and data protection

- [ ] Verify PDPL 151/2020 and Decree 816/2025 Arabic text, current transition/operational status and PDPC requirements.
- [ ] Confirm controller/processor roles, lawful purposes/bases, notices at collection, consent capture only where consent is the selected basis, data subject rights, contact and response procedures.
- [ ] Confirm DPO, processing records, licenses/permits and breach notification requirements for this entity and activities.
- [ ] Verify actual processors, sub-processors, data locations, international transfers and required approvals/contractual safeguards.
- [ ] Validate data categories, retention periods, backup deletion, account deletion/export coverage and legal retention exceptions.
- [ ] Determine child-data treatment and whether age/guardian consent or additional safeguards are required.
- [ ] Verify production cookies, tracking tags, browser storage and any direct marketing; approve consent and preference controls.

## Contract and technical evidence

- [ ] Confirm terms are linked and presented before signup/order acceptance; define explicit acceptance and store accepted version/time as appropriate.
- [ ] Confirm Article 38 order correction and durable confirmation behavior; ensure order notice matches offer and contains required information.
- [ ] Confirm guest checkout confirmation, secure order lookup and privacy request verification.
- [ ] Confirm payment methods/refund routes, courier, delivery area/charges/estimates, loss/damage route, pre-order commercial terms and swap support.
- [ ] Confirm account suspension, user-content license, report/appeal/takedown and dispute procedures are actually operable.
- [ ] Confirm current policies are linked in all actual registration, checkout, account deletion and relevant collection points.

## Final approval

- [ ] Compare EN and AR line-by-line; counsel approves Arabic and controls language precedence.
- [ ] Remove all `TODO(confirm)` editorial placeholders only after evidence/decision.
- [ ] Reconcile website text, checkout, order confirmations, support scripts and policies.
- [ ] Record counsel name, jurisdiction, review date, approved versions and scope.
- [ ] Do not mark “legally approved” until counsel expressly approves the final exact versions.
