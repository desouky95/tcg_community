# Topps Flagship Premier League 2026/27: A New Season, a New Set to Chase

The Premier League is back, and so is the hunt for a fresh season of football cards. Topps Flagship Premier League 2026/27 brings together familiar stars, promising young players, club-focused cards, and a wide range of inserts for collectors to discover.

Whether you collect one club, follow a favourite player, or enjoy building a complete set, this release gives you plenty of ways to make the season your own.

## What’s in the collection?

The release is built around a 300-card base set, including a 20-card Future Stars subset and players from all 20 Premier League clubs. It’s a natural starting point for a new binder, and a good way to keep track of the names shaping the season.

Beyond the base cards, collectors can look for inserts such as **Gold Lion**, **Chrome Classics**, **8-Bit Ballers**, **Retro Threads**, **All Kings**, and **Diamond Rookies**. Some cards are easier to find than others, with short prints, parallels, autographs, and relic cards adding extra variety to the chase.

## For club collectors and rising stars

Following one team? Start with its cards in the base set, then see which inserts and parallels you can add along the way. The Future Stars subset puts emerging talent in focus, while Diamond Rookies highlights a smaller group of young players collectors may want to watch.

The set also looks beyond the current squads. Chrome Classics and anniversary autograph cards bring Premier League history into the collection, making room in the same binder for today’s favourites and players who helped define earlier eras.

## Choosing how to collect

Packs offer the surprise of discovering cards one at a time. A box can help you make faster progress, while singles and trades can help fill the specific gaps in your checklist. If you’re opening packs with friends, swapping duplicates is part of the fun—and a simple way to get closer to completing your club or base set.

Before buying, check the product format and its contents. Value and Hobby boxes have different configurations and exclusive cards, and the odds vary by card type. A checklist is useful for identifying what you’ve pulled and deciding what you want to chase next.

## Start with the cards you love

You don’t need to chase every rare card to enjoy the collection. Pick a club, player, design, or subset that catches your eye, then build from there. The best collection is the one that keeps you connected to the football you love.
